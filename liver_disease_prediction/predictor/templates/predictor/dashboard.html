Ten Clinical Features Catalog
Exact Model Feature Schema
1. Age
Patient age in years (Range 1-120).
2. Gender
Biological sex (Encoded: Male=1, Female=0).
3. Total Bilirubin (TB)
Serum bilirubin in mg/dL. Elevated in jaundice and liver dysfunction.
4. Direct Bilirubin (DB)
Conjugated bilirubin in mg/dL. Indicates biliary obstruction or hepatic injury.
5. Alkaline Phosphotase (Alkphos)
Enzyme activity in IU/L. Marker for bile duct disorders.
6. Alamine Aminotransferase (SGPT)
Transaminase enzyme in IU/L. Highly specific indicator of acute liver injury.
7. Aspartate Aminotransferase (SGOT)
Enzyme activity in IU/L. Reflects hepatocellular injury and cellular damage.
8. Total Proteins (TP)
Total serum proteins in g/dL. Reflects liver biosynthetic capacity.
9. Albumin (ALB)
Major liver-synthesized plasma protein in g/dL. Decreases in chronic disease.
10. Albumin & Globulin Ratio (A/G)
Ratio between albumin and globulin proteins. Evaluates systemic dysproteinemia.
